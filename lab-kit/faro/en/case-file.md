# Faro case file

Faro Training is a **fictional** training centre with 14 people that runs courses for local companies. Every module of the course adds a new case at Faro. Never practise with your real accounts, devices or documents.

## What Faro uses every day

- Company email at `mail.faro.example`
- Shared folder "Clients" with the reports
- Team laptops
- Personal phones used for work
- Website `faro.example` with event photos

## Internal rules

1. Support never asks for passwords or codes by message.
2. Reports are shared with named people, never through open links.
3. Before replacing a file, confirm a copy exists.
4. Client reports are kept 12 months; nightly backups are kept 30 days.
5. Only the approved AI assistant may receive internal documents; restricted documents never go to any assistant.
6. Information categories: public, internal, restricted. Every document has an owner.

## Domains

All domains end in `.example`, a name reserved for documentation (RFC 2606). They do not point to real sites.
